- Operating system and version
- Unique device identifiers

#### Usage Data
- App features used
- Tasks completed
- Events viewed
- Time spent in app
- Navigation patterns
- Game progress (points, badges, streaks)

#### Technical Data
- IP address

3. **Legal Requirements**
   - To comply with legal processes
   - To enforce our Terms of Service
   - To protect rights, property, or safety
   - In response to lawful requests by authorities

4. **Business Transfers**
   - In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale
   - Users will be notified before data transfer

### What We Don't Share

- Your messages and conversations (private)
- Your relationship details (confidential)

- Personal identifiable information for marketing
