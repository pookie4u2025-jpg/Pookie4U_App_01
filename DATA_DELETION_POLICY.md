# Pookie4u Data Deletion Policy

**Effective Date**: November 4, 2025  
**Last Updated**: November 4, 2025

## Overview

At Pookie4u, we respect your right to control your personal data. This Data Deletion Policy outlines how users can request deletion of their account and associated data from our services.

## How to Delete Your Account

### In-App Deletion

1. Open the Pookie4u app
2. Go to **Profile** → **Settings**
3. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings screen
4. Tap **"Delete Account"** (below the Logout button)
5. Read the warning carefully
6. Confirm deletion twice for safety
7. Your account will be permanently deleted immediately

### Alternative Methods

If you're unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by:
- **Email**: Send a request to support@pookie4u.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request"
- Include your registered email address
- We will process your request within 30 days

## What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is **permanently removed**:

### Immediately Deleted
- ✅ User account (email, password hash)
- ✅ Profile information (name, picture, bio)
- ✅ Partner profile data
- ✅ Relationship mode settings
- ✅ All tasks (daily, weekly, completed)
- ✅ Custom events
- ✅ Messages and conversation history
- ✅ Game progress (points, badges, streaks, levels)
- ✅ Authentication sessions and tokens
- ✅ Push notification tokens
- ✅ Feedback submissions
- ✅ Referral data (both as referrer and referee)

### Subscription Data
- Active subscriptions: Cancelled immediately
- Subscription history: Retained for 90 days for refund/dispute purposes, then deleted
- Payment information: Handled by Razorpay (see their privacy policy)

### Data We May Retain

For legal and business purposes, we may retain the following **anonymized** data:
- Aggregated analytics (no personal identifiers)
- Transaction records (for tax/audit purposes, as required by law)
- Data required for legal compliance or dispute resolution

**Important**: We do NOT sell or share your deleted data. Once deleted, personal data cannot be recovered.

## Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention After Deletion Request |
|-----------|----------------------------------|
| Personal Profile Data | Deleted immediately |
| Authentication Data | Deleted immediately |
| Game Progress | Deleted immediately |
| Tasks & Events | Deleted immediately |
| Subscription Records | 90 days (for refunds/disputes) |
| Anonymized Analytics | Retained indefinitely |

## Before You Delete

Please note that **account deletion is permanent and irreversible**. You will lose:
- All relationship data and milestones
- Task history and achievements
- Points, badges, and current streak
- Custom events and reminders
- Any active subscription benefits

### Download Your Data

Before deleting your account, you can request a copy of your data:
- Email support@pookie4u.com with subject "Data Download Request"
- We'll send your data within 30 days in JSON or CSV format

## Children's Privacy

Pookie4u is not intended for users under 13 years of age. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us immediately at support@pookie4u.com, and we will delete the account.

## Third-Party Services

### OAuth Providers (Google, Emergent)
When you delete your Pookie4u account, we remove all connections to OAuth providers. However:
- Your Google/Emergent account remains active
- To revoke Pookie4u's access to your Google account:
  1. Go to [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)
  2. Find "Pookie4u" and click "Remove Access"

### Payment Provider (Razorpay)
- Pookie4u does not store your payment details
- All payment data is managed by Razorpay
- See [Razorpay Privacy Policy](https://razorpay.com/privacy/) for their data practices

## International Users

### GDPR (European Users)
If you're in the EU/EEA, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Rectify incorrect data
- Delete your data ("Right to be Forgotten")
- Port your data to another service
- Object to data processing

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@pookie4u.com

### CCPA (California Users)
If you're a California resident, you have the right to:
- Know what personal data we collect
- Delete your personal data
- Opt-out of data sales (we don't sell data)

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@pookie4u.com

## Data Deletion Verification

After account deletion:
1. You'll receive a confirmation email
2. All app sessions will be logged out
3. You won't be able to log in with your credentials
4. Your profile won't appear in searches or recommendations

## Reactivation

**Account deletion is permanent**. You cannot reactivate a deleted account. If you wish to use Pookie4u again, you must:
- Create a new account
- Start from scratch (no data recovery)
- Repurchase any subscriptions

## Data Breaches

In the event of a data breach:
- We will notify affected users within 72 hours
- Deleted accounts are excluded from breach notifications (no data to breach)
- See our Privacy Policy for full breach notification procedures

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Data Deletion Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted:
- In the app (Settings → Privacy Policy)
- On our website (pookie4u.com)
- Via email notification (for significant changes)

Continued use of Pookie4u after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
