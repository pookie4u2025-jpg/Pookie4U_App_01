# Account Deletion Guide for Pookie4u

## Quick Guide: How to Delete Your Account

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open Pookie4u App**
   - Launch the app on your device
   - Make sure you're logged in

2. **Go to Settings**
   - Tap on **Profile** tab (bottom navigation)
   - Tap on **Settings** icon (top right)
   - Or tap "Settings" option in profile menu

3. **Scroll to Bottom**
   - Scroll all the way down
   - Find the **"Delete Account"** button
   - It's below the red "Logout" button

4. **Confirm Deletion**
   - Read the warning carefully
   - Tap **"Delete Account"**
   - Confirm deletion (you'll be asked twice)

5. **Account Deleted**
   - Your account is permanently deleted
   - You'll be logged out automatically
   - You'll receive a confirmation email

### Visual Guide

```
Profile → Settings → Scroll Down → Delete Account → Confirm (2x) → Done!
```

- 📊 Aggregated analytics (no personal identifiers)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I recover my account after deletion?

**No.** Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted:
- You cannot log back in
- Your data cannot be recovered
- You must create a new account to use Pookie4u again

### What about my subscription?

- **Active subscriptions** are cancelled immediately
- **Refunds** follow Razorpay's refund policy
- **Subscription history** retained for 90 days
- Contact support@pookie4u.com for refund requests

### Can I download my data first?

**Yes!** Email support@pookie4u.com with:
- Subject: "Data Download Request"
- Your registered email address
- We'll send your data within 30 days

### Will my partner's account be affected?

**No.** Deleting your account does not affect your partner's account (if they have one). However:
- They may see your profile as "deleted user"
- Shared events/tasks will be removed
- Consider informing them before deleting

### How long does deletion take?

**Immediate.** Most data is deleted instantly when you confirm. Some data (like backups) may take up to 90 days to fully purge.

### Will I receive confirmation?

**Yes.** You'll receive:
1. In-app confirmation message
2. Email confirmation to your registered email
3. Automatic logout from all devices

### Can I delete my account if I signed up with Google?

**Yes.** The deletion process is the same regardless of how you signed up:
- Email/Password
- Google OAuth
- Emergent OAuth

Note: Deleting your Pookie4u account does NOT delete your Google account.

### What if I can't access the app?

If you can't log in or access the app:

**Email Method:**
1. Send email to support@pookie4u.com
2. Subject: "Account Deletion Request - Cannot Access App"
3. Include your registered email
4. We'll process within 30 days

**Security Verification:**
We may ask verification questions to confirm identity:
- Date of account creation
- Last 4 digits of phone number
- Partner's name (if applicable)

### Is there a waiting period?

**No waiting period.** Deletion is immediate, but you'll see two confirmation dialogs to prevent accidental deletion.

### What about data on Razorpay?

Pookie4u does NOT store payment information. All payment data is managed by Razorpay:

- **To delete payment data**: Contact Razorpay support
- **Pookie4u Privacy Policy**: https://razorpay.com/privacy/
- **Transaction history**: Retained by Razorpay per their policy

### What about Google OAuth access?

Deleting your Pookie4u account removes our connection to Google, but:

**To fully revoke access:**
1. Go to [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)
2. Find "Pookie4u"
3. Click "Remove Access"

### I'm having second thoughts. Can I stop the deletion?

**Only before confirming.** Once you click the final "Yes, Delete Forever" button, deletion begins immediately and cannot be stopped.

**If you haven't confirmed yet:**
- Click "Cancel" on any confirmation dialog
- Your account remains active

## Troubleshooting

### "Delete Account" button not working

1. **Check internet connection**: Deletion requires connectivity
2. **Update the app**: Ensure you have the latest version
3. **Restart the app**: Close and reopen
4. **Try again**: Sometimes a temporary glitch
5. **Contact support**: If problem persists

### Error: "Failed to delete account"

**Possible reasons:**
- Network timeout
- Server error
- Active subscription lock

**Solutions:**
1. Try again in a few minutes
2. Cancel subscription first (Settings > Subscription)
3. Contact support@pookie4u.com

### I deleted my account but still receive emails

**Wait 24-48 hours** for email systems to update. If emails continue after 48 hours:

1. Click "Unsubscribe" in email footer
2. Email privacy@pookie4u.com
3. Report as spam (last resort)
